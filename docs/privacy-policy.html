Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Remotify (“we”, “the app”) is an Android TV remote control app. This policy explains what information the app uses and how third-party services work.

Information we collect

  • TV connection data — TV names, IP addresses, and pairing certificates you save are stored locally on your device only (AsyncStorage). We do not operate a backend server for your remote sessions.
  • Local network — The app discovers Android TVs on your Wi‑Fi using network discovery. Data stays on your device and local network.
  • Advertising ID — The free version shows ads via Google AdMob. Google may use your device’s advertising identifier to serve and measure ads. See Google’s ad policies.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — banner and occasional full-screen ads. AdMob privacy information
  • Google Play — if you install or update the app from the Play Store.

Children

Remotify is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Reset advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Remove saved TVs in the app at any time.
  • Uninstall the app to delete local data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
